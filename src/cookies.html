1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. Similar technologies — such as browser local storage — work in a comparable way. They let a site remember your actions and preferences (like staying signed in) over time.

2. How we use them

We keep our use of cookies deliberately minimal. We use essential cookies and storage that are required for the site to work — chiefly to keep you signed in — and, only with your consent, optional cookies that help us understand how the site is used so we can improve it. We do not use cookies for advertising.

  • Authentication / session — set by our backend provider to keep you signed in and secure your session. [provider · cookie names · duration]
  • Consent preference — remembers whether you accepted or declined optional cookies (stored in your browser's local storage as rib-cookie-consent), so we don't ask again on every visit.

Optional (consent-based)

  • Analytics / usage — if enabled, helps us measure and improve the Service. These are only set after you accept. [provider · cookie names · duration]

4. Local storage

In addition to cookies, we use your browser's local storage for small preferences that keep the experience smooth — for example your cookie-consent choice and, when you choose "keep me signed in", your session preference. Local storage stays on your device and is not sent to us automatically.

5. Third-party cookies

When you sign in with Google, GitHub or Apple, when you buy rcoin through Stripe's checkout, or when scripts load from our content-delivery provider, those third parties may set their own cookies under their own policies. We do not control those cookies. See each provider's privacy policy for detail.

6. Managing your choices

When you first visit, a banner lets you accept or decline optional cookies. You can change your mind at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser, which resets the banner on your next visit. You can also block or delete cookies through your browser settings — note that blocking essential cookies may stop you from staying signed in.

7. Changes

We may update this policy as our use of cookies changes. We will revise the "Last updated" date above when we do.

8. Contact

Questions about cookies can be sent to privacy@runinback.com. For how we handle personal data more broadly, see our Privacy Policy.
