The engine

A trustless loop from pairing to payout.

No manual payouts and no treasury on your side. Runinback runs every paid match through the same verifiable four-step path, designed to settle on-chain on Base.

01

Matchmaking — pair & agree

Both players connect a wallet (any Base wallet or an embedded account) and confirm the game, rules and entry fee through a challenge overlay. Anti-collusion heuristics run before a match is ever created.

02

Escrow — hold the entry fees

When the match starts, both entry fees move into an escrow contract. The funds are provably locked on-chain — neither player, nor your game, nor Runinback can move them until a verified result arrives.

03

Verification — prove the result

Your game server submits a signed result. The Runinback oracle validates the signature and opens a short dispute window backed by cryptographic evidence, so a bad actor can't fake an outcome.

04

Settlement — pay the winner

Once verified, the escrow releases the full prize to the winner automatically — typically in about a second. If a match is voided, funds are refunded to both players along the same trustless path.

Why trustless matters

You keep the players. We remove the risk.

Non-custodial by design

You never touch player funds, so you avoid money-transmitter exposure and the operational weight of a payments stack.

Verifiable on-chain

Every held prize and payout is a public, auditable transaction. Players can confirm the prize is real before they ever queue up.

Instant & automatic

No support tickets, no batch payouts. The oracle settles the moment a result is verified, paid out on Base.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

In production, entry fees are designed to sit in an on-chain escrow contract on Base that neither player nor Runinback can move until the result is verified. Today the platform runs in test mode: rcoin balances are simulated, have no cash value and are recorded in Runinback's own ledger.

Results are submitted as signed messages from your authenticated game server, then validated by the oracle with a dispute window. Unsigned or contradictory results are rejected before any payout.

Runinback is built for Base: entry fees and prizes will settle in USDC on Base. Today the platform runs in test mode with simulated rcoin (1 rcoin = $1), and card or crypto top-ups are in test mode until legal review clears real-money play.

If a match is voided or times out without a valid result, the escrow follows its refund path and returns each entry fee to the original player automatically.
