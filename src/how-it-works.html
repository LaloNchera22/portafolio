How it works

From challenge to payout, step by step.

Every paid match on Runinback follows the same four steps. Here is exactly what happens to your entry fee, and how the winner is decided.

01

Matchmaking — pick how you compete

Open a paid table in one of the six built-in games, post a 1v1 challenge for any game you play elsewhere (to the open lobby or straight to a rival), or join a tournament. The game, the rules and the entry fee are shown before you commit, and both players pay the same amount.

02

Escrow — hold the entry fees

When the match is on, both entry fees move from your available balance into escrow, shown as “In play” in your wallet. Neither player can take them back mid-match. A table nobody has joined yet can still be cancelled for a full refund.

03

Verification — decide the winner fairly

In built-in games, the server checks every move against the rules and ends the game itself; each move has a 10-minute clock, so nobody can stall. Challenges settle when both players report the same result. Tournament results get a 24-hour review window in which any entrant can dispute them.

04

Settlement — pay the winner

The winner receives both entry fees, with no cut taken from the prize. A draw refunds each player, and a challenge left without a matching result can be voided for a full refund after two hours.

Why it's fair

Skill decides. The rules are the same for everyone.

Skill, not luck

Paid tables are limited to games with no dice and no hidden cards, so the better player wins. Your moves are validated by the server, not by your opponent's browser.

No cut on prizes

The only fee is 5% when you buy rcoin. Entry fees, prizes and withdrawals are free, so the winner takes the whole prize.

A record you can check

Every entry fee, prize and refund appears in your wallet activity. The ranking counts only server-verified games, so it can't be padded with reported results.
