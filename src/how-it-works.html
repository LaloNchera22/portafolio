How it works

From entry fee to payout, step by step.

Every Runinback tournament follows the same four steps. Here is exactly what happens to your entry fee, how each match is decided and how the prize is paid.

01

Matchmaking — join a tournament

Pick a Wild Rift sit & go of 4 or 8 players, free or from 1 to 50 rcoin. It starts the moment it fills, the bracket is drawn at random, and each 1v1 opens a room with the custom-game name and password; the end screen settles the result.

02

Escrow — hold the entry fees

Your entry fee goes into the prize pool when you join. Until the tournament fills you can leave and get it back, and if it doesn't fill within 24 hours every entry fee is refunded. Once it starts, the pool stays in escrow until the final is decided.

03

Verification — decide every match fairly

Both players get 15 minutes to join the lobby and press Ready; a player who doesn't loses by walkover. After the match, both report the result: a matching report settles it at once, and if the opponent doesn't respond within 15 minutes, the report stands. A dispute holds a deposit of 10% of the entry fee and goes to the Runinback team with the room chat and captures taken in the app.

04

Settlement — pay the winners

Winners advance on their own. When the final is confirmed, Runinback keeps 10% of the pool and pays the rest at once: 70% to the champion and 30% to the runner-up. If a tournament ends with no winner, every entry fee is refunded.

Why it's fair

Skill decides. The rules are the same for everyone.

Skill, not luck

You win by beating your opponent in the game itself, match by match. There are no odds and no house side: Runinback never plays against you.

One clear fee

Runinback keeps 10% of each paid tournament's pool, and 90% goes to the players. Buying rcoin has a 5% fee; withdrawals are free.

Honesty pays, lying costs

A false dispute loses its deposit to the other player and counts on the record everyone sees. New accounts are capped at 25 rcoin until they finish 3 matches.
