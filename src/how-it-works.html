How it works

Every rule, every minute, every rcoin.

How a Runinback tournament runs from the tap that seats you to the prize in your wallet: the bracket, the clocks, the fees and what happens when players disagree.

The bracket

Single elimination, 4 or 8 players.

  • A random draw when it fills

    The bracket is drawn at random the moment the last seat fills, so nobody picks their opponent. A 4-player bracket plays semifinals and a final; an 8-player bracket adds quarterfinals.

  • Every match is a 1v1 custom game

    Each match opens its own room with a Wild Rift custom-game lobby name and password. The first player listed hosts, the other joins, and you play it out. Win and you advance automatically.

  • Quick Play or your own tournament

    Quick Play seats you in the oldest open bracket of the tier you pick, and opens a new one when every bracket is full. You can also create a custom tournament with its own name, size and entry fee and share it.

  • Leave any time before it starts

    Until the bracket fills you can leave for a full refund. If it doesn't fill within 24 hours, every entry fee is refunded automatically.

Timings

Three clocks keep every bracket moving.

No match waits on a player who walked away. Each clock is shown in the match room as it runs.

Ready check5 min

Starts when your match opens. Both players press Ready. If you're ready and your opponent isn't, you advance by walkover; if neither is ready, both are out of that match.

Confirm window10 min

Starts when the first player reports the result. The opponent confirms or disputes. Matching reports settle at once; silence lets the first report stand when the window closes.

After a verified end screen3 min

When your uploaded end screen clearly shows your win and both Riot IDs, the opponent's window shrinks to 3 minutes. They can still dispute in that time.

Fees and payouts

One fee, shown before you join.

Runinback keeps 10% of each paid tournament's pool. The other 90% is prize: 70% to the champion, 30% to the runner-up.

  • Entry fees are paid in rcoin

    1 rcoin = $1. You buy rcoin by card through Stripe with a 5% purchase fee, so $100 buys 95 rcoin. Withdrawals have no fee.

  • Prizes are paid when the final is confirmed

    The champion and runner-up are credited straight to their wallets the moment the final settles. Every entry fee and prize shows up in your wallet activity.

  • No winner, no fee

    If a tournament ends without a winner, every entry fee is refunded. Free tiers cost nothing and pay no prize.

Results and disputes

Players report. Screens are checked. People decide disputes.

  1. 1

    Both players report

    After the game, each player reports who won. If the reports agree, the match settles immediately and the winner advances.

  2. 2

    Upload the end screen

    Your end-of-match screenshot is read automatically: is it a Wild Rift end screen, who won, and are both Riot IDs on it? A clear match with your report shortens the confirm window to 3 minutes. A screenshot never settles a match on its own, and one already used in another match is flagged.

  3. 3

    Silence confirms

    If your opponent doesn't confirm or dispute before the window closes, your report stands.

  4. 4

    Disagree? Open a dispute

    The match goes to human review with the room chat and both players' screenshots. In paid matches a dispute holds a deposit of 10% of the entry fee (at least 1 rcoin): you get it back if you're right, and a rejected dispute's deposit goes to the other player.

Your Riot ID

One player, one Name#TAG.

  • Required to enter

    Link your Riot ID in the console before you join a tournament. Your opponent uses it to find you in the custom game, and the end screen is checked against it.

  • Checked with Riot

    We confirm the Riot ID exists, and a verified ID shows a badge. Each Riot ID can belong to only one Runinback account.

  • Locked while you compete

    You can't change your Riot ID while you have an open entry or a live match, so the name in the bracket is the name in the game.

  • Coming: Riot Sign-On

    Signing in with your Riot account will prove you own the ID, not just that it exists. It isn't available yet.
