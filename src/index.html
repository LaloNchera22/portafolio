How a match works

From tap to prize in six steps.

  1. Link your Riot ID

    Add your Name#TAG once. Your opponent uses it to find you, and it's required to enter.

  2. Pick a tier and tap

    Choose an entry fee and 4 or 8 players. The bracket is drawn at random and starts the moment the last seat fills.

  3. Ready check 5 min

    When your match opens, both players press Ready. Miss it and you lose by walkover.

  4. Play the 1v1

    One player hosts a Wild Rift custom game with the lobby name and password from the match room. Play it out.

  5. Report the result 10 min

    Both players report and upload the end screen. Matching reports settle at once; a verified end screen cuts the wait to 3 minutes.

  6. Get paid

    Winners advance on their own. When the final is confirmed, the champion and runner-up are paid straight to their wallets.

Fair play

Results both players can stand behind.

Read the dispute rules

  • Both players report

    When the two reports agree, the match settles right away. If one player stays silent, the other's report stands when the 10-minute window closes.

  • The end screen is checked

    We read the end-of-match screenshot automatically and compare it with both Riot IDs. A clear match speeds things up; it never decides a result on its own, and a screenshot reused from another match is flagged.

  • Disputes go to a person

    Disagree with a report and the match goes to human review with the room chat and screenshots. A dispute holds a 10% deposit that a false claim loses.

  • One player, one Riot ID

    A Riot ID belongs to one account and locks while you're in a bracket. New accounts can enter up to 25 rcoin until they finish 3 matches.
