How it works

From invite link to prize.

Every match is a 1v1 Wild Rift custom game. Link your Riot ID (Name#TAG) once; you need it to enter.

  1. 01

    Create or join

    A host creates the tournament and shares its link. Players open it, see the entry fee and prize, and join.

  2. 02

    The bracket is drawn

    It starts when the last seat fills, or earlier when the host starts it with 4 or more players. Seeds are random.

  3. 03

    The host opens the lobby

    For each match the host creates the Wild Rift custom game and posts the lobby code or a screenshot in the match room.

  4. 04

    Play, then the host decides

    You play the 1v1 and can upload the end screen. The host picks the winner, who advances; the loser is out.

  5. 05

    24 hours to appeal 24h

    After the final, any player can appeal a decision. An appeal holds the prize until a person on our team reviews it.

  6. 06

    Everyone gets paid

    The champion's prize and the host's commission land in their wallets. Match chats are deleted once it's all settled.

Fair play

Hosts decide. People review.

  • Hosts can't play

    The person who decides the matches can't enter their own tournament.

  • Appeals go to a person

    Our team reviews appeals with the match chat and screenshots. A wrong call moves the prize and the host's commission to the right winner.

  • Quick calls get checked

    A walkover needs a 10-minute wait and a reason. Suspiciously fast decisions are flagged, and new hosts have a longer appeal window.

  • Hosts who stall lose it

    A bracket that stops moving for 24 hours is cancelled and every entry fee is refunded. Hosts with repeated bad calls can only run free brackets.
