How a match works

Three steps. One fair loop.

Every Runinback match follows the same verifiable path, from finding a rival to paying the winner.

Matchmaking

Find a rival, agree the rules, set the entry fee.

Challenge a friend or pick an open match from the lobby. Both players see the game, the rules and the entry fee before anything starts.

  • Open lobby and direct 1v1 challenges
  • Fixed entry fees from 1 rcoin, the same for both players
  • Tournaments with a 24-hour result review
Explore matchmaking →
Escrow

Entry fees held safe from the first move.

Both entry fees move into escrow when the match starts, shown as “In play” in your wallet. Neither player can take them back until the result is settled.

  • Nobody can pull out mid-match
  • Every entry fee and prize in your wallet activity
  • Full refund for a draw, a voided match or an unjoined table
Explore escrow →
Settlement

The result is verified and the winner is paid.

Built-in games are checked move by move on the server, which ends the game and pays the winner. Challenges in other games settle when both players report the same result.

  • Server-verified moves and a 10-minute turn clock
  • Challenges pay out only when both reports match
  • No cut taken from the prize

In your console today

Everything you need to compete.

27 games, free practice

Warm up against the house bot in any game, as often as you like. Six of them — Tic-Tac-Toe, Connect Four, Reversi, Checkers, Dots & Boxes and Mancala — also have paid tables.

1v1 challenges

Post a challenge to the open lobby or send it straight to a rival, for any game you play, with an entry fee from 1 to 1,000 rcoin.

Tournaments

Create or join events with a shared entry fee. The prize is paid after a 24-hour review window in which entrants can dispute the result.

Weekly and all-time ranking

Climb a leaderboard of net rcoin won that counts only server-verified games. The weekly board resets every Monday.

Your rcoin wallet

Buy rcoin with a 5% fee on the way in, see every entry fee and prize in your activity, and withdraw with no fee.

Developer tools

Create projects, issue and revoke API keys, and call the REST API for the game catalog and the verified ranking.
