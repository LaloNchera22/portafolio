How a tournament works

Three steps. One fair bracket.

Every Runinback tournament follows the same verifiable path, from the first match to paying the winners.

Matchmaking

Join a tournament, get your bracket.

Pick a Wild Rift sit & go of 4 or 8 players. It starts the moment it fills, the bracket is drawn at random, and every match opens its own room with the lobby details.

  • 4 or more players, free or from 1 to 500 rcoin
  • A random draw and a bracket everyone can see
Explore matchmaking →
Escrow

Entry fees held safe until the final.

Your entry fee goes into the prize pool when you join. Until the tournament fills you can leave and get it back; after that it stays in escrow until the final is decided.

  • Leave for a full refund before it starts
  • Full refund if it doesn't fill within 24 hours
  • Every entry fee and prize in your wallet activity
Explore escrow →
Settlement

Winners advance. The final pays out.

Both players report each match. A matching report settles it at once, and silence for 15 minutes confirms it; disputes go to the Runinback team with the room chat and in-app captures. When the final is confirmed, the prize is paid right away.

  • 90% of the pool is prize: 70% champion, 30% runner-up
  • A 10% platform fee, shown before you join
  • No-shows lose by walkover, so brackets keep moving
