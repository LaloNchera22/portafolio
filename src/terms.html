5. Test mode & rcoin

The Service currently runs in test mode. Balances are denominated in rcoin, an in-platform unit (1 rcoin is displayed as US$1). In test mode:

  • Runinback holds and records balances in its own ledger (it is custodial during test mode). Stakes locked in a match, challenge or tournament are held in escrow in that ledger until the result settles.

7. Matches, challenges & tournaments

  • Staked games played in the console are validated move by move by our servers, and the pot is settled automatically when the game ends or a player resigns.
  • Turn clock. Each move in a staked game must be made within the time shown (currently 10 minutes). If a player runs out of time, the opponent may claim the win, and otherwise the match is awarded to the opponent automatically.
  • Challenges (games played outside the console) are settled when both players report the same result. If the reports conflict, or no result is reported, the stakes stay in escrow and can be returned to both players after the period shown in the console; abandoned or unaccepted challenges expire and are refunded.
  • Tournaments. The organizer may not enter their own paid tournament, may not be declared the winner, and may only finish a tournament that has the minimum number of entrants. The prize is held for a review period after the organizer declares a winner, during which any entrant may dispute the result; disputed prizes are reviewed and may be paid, reassigned or refunded to entrants.
  • Rankings count only results validated by our servers. We may correct or remove results obtained through collusion, multiple accounts or other abuse.

8. Top-ups, refunds & chargebacks

rcoin top-ups are processed by our payment providers (card via Stripe; crypto via Coinbase Commerce). If a payment is refunded or disputed, we reverse the corresponding rcoin. If that rcoin was already used, the remaining balance is debited and the account may be restricted from staking and withdrawing until the shortfall is resolved.
