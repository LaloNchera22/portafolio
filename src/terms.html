6. Skill-based competition, fees & prizes

Tournaments offered through the Service are competitions decided by player skill in Wild Rift. There are two kinds: hosted tournaments, created and run by a player (the "host"), and Quick Play tournaments, which have no host (see section 7). You are responsible for understanding the rules of a tournament, including any rules text its host adds, before you pay an entry fee.

  • A 5% fee is charged once when you buy rcoin (for example, $100 buys 95 rcoin). There is no fee on withdrawals. Every fee is shown before you confirm an action.
  • Every entrant in a tournament pays the same entry fee. The prize pool is the entry fee multiplied by the number of players who actually entered.
  • Hosted tournaments: the champion receives 85% of the prize pool as the prize, the host receives 5% as a host commission, and Runinback keeps 10% as a platform fee. The host commission is paid only together with the prize, after the appeal window closes or any appeal is resolved (section 7).
  • Quick Play: Runinback keeps 10% of the prize pool as a platform fee; the remaining 90% is the prize, of which 70% goes to the champion and 30% to the runner-up when the final is confirmed.
  • In line with Riot Games' tournament policy, at least 70% of all entry fees collected for a tournament is paid out as prizes (85% in hosted tournaments, 90% in Quick Play). Tournaments with no entry fee carry no prize and no host commission.
  • Runinback does not guarantee the conduct of any other player or host, of the game or of any third-party service.
  • You are responsible for any taxes arising from your activity, including prizes and host commissions.

7. Tournaments & matches

Hosted tournaments

  • Creating a tournament. Any signed-in user can create a tournament of 4, 8, 16 or 32 players with an entry fee from 0 to 500 rcoin, optionally with rules text, and becomes its host. A tournament is either public (listed in the Service) or private (reachable only through its invite link or code). A host can run up to 3 tournaments at a time. Until a host has completed 3 hosted tournaments that were paid out without a strike, the entry fee they can set is capped (currently 25 rcoin).
  • Joining and starting. Players join through the listing or the invite link, and may leave for a full refund until the tournament starts. The host cannot enter their own tournament. A tournament starts automatically when all places are filled, or earlier when the host starts it with at least 4 players. The bracket is single elimination and drawn at random; if it is not full, some players receive a bye into the next round.
  • Matches and decisions. For each match the host creates the Wild Rift custom lobby and posts its code or a screenshot of it in the match room. Players may report that they won and upload the end-of-match screen; this is shown to the host but does not decide the match. The host decides every match: the winner advances and the loser is out. The host may award a walkover when a player has not shown up, or void the match when neither has, only after the room has been open for at least 10 minutes and with a written reason. Unusually quick decisions are flagged to our team.
  • Host obligations. By hosting you agree to run the tournament in good faith; to decide each match on the evidence available (the game, the players' reports, screenshots and the match chat), not on personal preference; not to use other accounts, or act with any player, to enter, influence or profit from your own tournament; and not to decide matches in your own interest or in the interest of anyone connected to you. If we find that a host has abused these duties, we may withhold or reverse the host commission, correct results, and restrict or suspend the host's account, in addition to the measures below.
  • Host record. A host receives a strike when an appeal overturns their decision, when all entry fees are refunded after an appeal, or when their tournament is voided for inactivity. A host with 3 or more strikes can only host tournaments with no entry fee.
  • Appeals. After the host decides the final, there is an appeal window before prizes are paid: 24 hours, or 72 hours for a paid tournament whose host has not yet completed 3 clean hosted tournaments. During the window any entrant may appeal once, giving a reason and optionally the match concerned. In a paid tournament an appeal holds a deposit of 10% of the entry fee (minimum 1 rcoin). An appeal holds the payout until people on our team resolve it, using the match chat, the host's decisions and reasons, the reports and screenshots. We may (a) let the result stand, in which case appeal deposits are kept by Runinback; (b) overturn it and pay the prize to the player we find to be the rightful winner, who also receives the host commission; or (c) refund every entry fee. In cases (b) and (c) appeal deposits are returned and the host receives a strike. Tournaments with no entry fee also have an appeal window.
  • Cancellations and refunds. The host may cancel a tournament before it starts. A hosted tournament that has not started within 7 days of its creation is cancelled. A tournament that has started but makes no progress for 24 hours is voided, and its host receives a strike. We may cancel any tournament that has not been paid out. In each of these cases every entry fee (and any open appeal deposit) is refunded and no prize or host commission is paid.

Quick Play

  • Tournaments are "sit & go" events of 4 or 8 players with no host that start when all places are filled. Quick Play places you in an open tournament of the entry fee and size you choose. Entrants may leave for a full refund until the tournament starts; a tournament that does not fill within 24 hours is cancelled and every entry fee is refunded. The bracket is single elimination and is drawn at random, and winners advance automatically.
  • Matches are 1v1 Wild Rift custom games. Each match has a room that shows the custom-lobby details; one player hosts the lobby and both join it with the Riot IDs linked to their accounts.
  • Ready check. Both players must mark themselves ready within the time shown in the room (currently 5 minutes). A player who is not ready in time loses the match by walkover.
  • Reporting results. Both players report the result. Matching reports settle the match at once. If one player reports and the other neither reports nor disputes within the confirm window shown in the room (currently 10 minutes), the report stands: silence confirms.
  • Disputes. A player may dispute a reported result by giving a reason and, in paid tournaments, placing a deposit of 10% of the entry fee (minimum 1 rcoin). Disputes are reviewed by people on our team, who look at the room chat, the reports, the screenshots and other evidence captured in the app, and decide the match. The automated screenshot check may inform that review but does not decide it. A rejected (false) dispute's deposit is lost and paid to the other player; an upheld dispute's deposit is returned. Until then the match stays undecided.

All tournaments

  • End-of-match screenshots. After reporting, a player may upload a screenshot of the Wild Rift end-of-match screen. The screenshot is read by an automated system that uses an AI model to recognise the players' names and the result shown. In Quick Play, if it clearly shows the reported winner and both players' Riot IDs, the opponent's confirm window is shortened to 3 minutes; the opponent can still dispute. In hosted tournaments the result of the check is shown to the host. A screenshot never settles, cancels or disputes a match on its own. A screenshot that contradicts a report, or that was already used in another match, is flagged for our team. Uploading an edited, reused or someone else's screenshot is a breach of these Terms. How screenshots are processed is described in our Privacy Policy.
  • Match chat. Each match room has its own chat, visible to the two players, the host of a hosted tournament and our team. Keep it to the match and be respectful. Chat messages and lobby screenshots are deleted once the tournament is finished or cancelled, after any appeal; they may be reviewed before then to resolve disputes and appeals.
  • Limits and restrictions. To protect fair play we apply rate limits on entries and tournament creation, allow one open Quick Play entry per tier, and cap the entry fees a new account can pay until it has completed a number of matches (currently 25 rcoin until 3 completed matches). Accounts with repeated false disputes or appeals, no-shows or host strikes, or that break these Terms, may be restricted from entering or hosting tournaments.
  • Rankings count only results confirmed through the Service. Matches decided by a host do not count towards rankings or completed matches. We may correct or remove results obtained through collusion, multiple accounts or other abuse.
