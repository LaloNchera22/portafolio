7. Tournaments & matches

  • Tournaments are "sit & go" events of 4 or 8 players that start when all places are filled. You can join through Quick Play, which places you in an open tournament of the entry fee and size you choose, or enter or create a custom tournament. Entrants may leave for a full refund until the tournament starts; a tournament that does not fill within 24 hours is cancelled and every entry fee is refunded. The bracket is single elimination and is drawn at random, and winners advance automatically.
  • Matches are 1v1 Wild Rift custom games. Each match has a room that shows the custom-lobby details; one player hosts the lobby and both join it with the Riot IDs linked to their accounts.
  • Ready check. Both players must mark themselves ready within the time shown in the room (currently 5 minutes). A player who is not ready in time loses the match by walkover.
  • Reporting results. Both players report the result. Matching reports settle the match at once. If one player reports and the other neither reports nor disputes within the confirm window shown in the room (currently 10 minutes), the report stands: silence confirms.
  • End-of-match screenshots. After reporting, a player may upload a screenshot of the Wild Rift end-of-match screen. The screenshot is read by an automated system that uses an AI model to recognise the players' names and the result shown. If it clearly shows the reported winner and both players' Riot IDs, the opponent's confirm window is shortened to 3 minutes; the opponent can still dispute. A screenshot never settles, cancels or disputes a match on its own. A screenshot that contradicts a report, or that was already used in another match, is flagged for our team. Uploading an edited, reused or someone else's screenshot is a breach of these Terms. How screenshots are processed is described in our Privacy Policy.
  • Disputes. A player may dispute a reported result by giving a reason and, in paid tournaments, placing a deposit of 10% of the entry fee (minimum 1 rcoin). Disputes are reviewed by people on our team, who look at the room chat, the reports, the screenshots and other evidence captured in the app, and decide the match. The automated screenshot check may inform that review but does not decide it. A rejected (false) dispute's deposit is lost and paid to the other player; an upheld dispute's deposit is returned. Until then the match stays undecided.
  • Limits and restrictions. To protect fair play we apply rate limits on entries, allow one open Quick Play entry per tier, and cap the entry fees a new account can pay until it has completed a number of matches (currently 25 rcoin until 3 completed matches). Accounts with repeated false disputes or no-shows, or that break these Terms, may be restricted from entering tournaments.
  • Rankings count only results confirmed through the Service. We may correct or remove results obtained through collusion, multiple accounts or other abuse.
