7. Tournaments, matches & friendlies

  • Tournaments start when all places are filled. Entrants may leave for a full refund until then; a tournament that does not fill within 24 hours is cancelled and every entry fee is refunded. The bracket is drawn at random and winners advance automatically.
  • Match rooms. Each match must start within the ready check shown in the room (currently 15 minutes); a player who is not ready in time loses by walkover. Both players report the result. A matching report settles the match; if one player reports and the other does not respond within the time shown (currently 15 minutes), the report stands.
  • Disputes. A player may dispute a reported result by giving a reason and, in paid tournaments, placing a deposit of 10% of the entry fee (minimum 1 rcoin). Our team reviews the room chat and the captures taken in the app and decides the match. A rejected dispute's deposit is paid to the other player; an upheld dispute's deposit is returned. Accounts with repeated false claims or no-shows may be restricted.
  • Friendlies are free 1v1 matches that use the same rooms. They carry no entry fee and no prize; a disputed friendly ends with no result.
  • Built-in games played online in the console are free friendlies. Our servers validate every move, and each move must be made within the time shown (currently 10 minutes); a player who runs out of time loses the game.
  • Rankings count only results validated by our servers. We may correct or remove results obtained through collusion, multiple accounts or other abuse.

8. Top-ups, refunds & chargebacks

If that rcoin was already used, the remaining balance is debited and the account may be restricted from staking and withdrawing until the shortfall is resolved.
