1. Who we are

Runinback ("we", "us") is a skill-based competition platform for Wild Rift, operated by [legal entity name], [registered address]. For the purposes of applicable data-protection law, the data controller is [controller name]. This policy explains how we handle personal data across our website, accounts, console and tournaments (the "Service").

2. Data we collect

Data you give us

  • Account data — username, email address and password (stored only as a secure hash by our authentication provider), and your confirmation that you meet the age and eligibility requirements.
  • Authentication data — when you sign in with Google, GitHub or Apple, we receive a basic identifier and email from that provider.
  • Profile & settings — optional bio, country and avatar, your notification and privacy choices, and any responsible-play limits you set.
  • Riot ID — the Riot ID (Name#TAG) of the Wild Rift account you link, which is required to enter tournaments. A copy of each player's Riot ID is kept with every match room they play.
  • Tournament & match data — tournament entries, brackets, ready checks, result reports, disputes and their reasons, messages in match-room chat, and the screenshots and other evidence you upload (see section 7).
  • Payment & balance data — rcoin purchases, entry fees, prizes, refunds and deposits recorded in our ledger. Card payments are handled by Stripe; we do not receive or store your full card number.
  • Communications — messages you send us through forms or email.

Data we collect automatically

  • Usage & device data — pages viewed, approximate region, browser and device type, and similar technical information.
  • Cookies & local storage — see our Cookie Policy for detail and your choices.
  • Error reports — when the console hits an unexpected error, a technical report (error message, page address and browser details) may be sent to us so we can fix it.
  • Fair-play records — entry rate limits, completed matches, no-shows, disputes lost and any account restrictions.

Data we receive from others

  • Riot Games — when you link a Riot ID, we may check it with Riot Games' account API. If the Riot ID exists, Riot returns its game name, tag line and a permanent player identifier (PUUID), which we store with your Riot ID and the date it was checked. This confirms the Riot ID exists; it does not prove who owns it.
  • Stripe — the status of your card payments, refunds and chargebacks.
  • Identity providers — Google, GitHub or Apple, when you choose to sign in with them.

During test mode, rcoin balances are simulated and recorded in our own ledger, and card purchases run in Stripe's test mode; see the Terms.

3. How we use it

  • To create and secure your account and sign you in.
  • To provide, maintain and improve the Service, including running tournaments, brackets and match rooms, holding entry fees and paying prizes.
  • To check match results, including the automated reading of end-of-match screenshots described in section 7, and to review disputes.
  • To apply rate limits, entry caps for new accounts, responsible-play limits and account restrictions.
  • To communicate with you about your account, support requests and important changes.
  • To detect, prevent and investigate fraud, cheating and abuse.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

4. Legal bases

Where data-protection law requires a legal basis, we rely on: performance of a contract (to run your account and the Service), legitimate interests (to secure and improve the Service and prevent abuse), consent (for optional cookies and any marketing), and legal obligation where the law requires it. You can withdraw consent at any time.

5. Cookies

We use essential cookies to keep you signed in and to run the Service, and — only with your consent — optional cookies to understand how the Service is used. You can accept or decline optional cookies from the banner shown on your first visit, and change your choice at any time. Full detail is in our Cookie Policy.

6. Sharing & processors

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with service providers who process it on our behalf under contract, including:

  • Authentication & database — our backend provider, which stores account and profile data.
  • Hosting & content delivery — the infrastructure that serves the site.
  • Payments — Stripe, which processes card purchases of rcoin.
  • Automated screenshot checks — Anthropic, which provides the AI model (Claude) that reads end-of-match screenshots on our behalf, as a processor (see section 7).
  • Riot Games — when we check a Riot ID, we send the game name and tag line to Riot Games' account API.
  • Identity providers — Google, GitHub and Apple, when you choose to sign in with them.

Other players in your tournaments and matches see your username and your Riot ID, and the other player in a match room sees what you post in its chat and your reports. What appears on your public profile and leaderboards follows your privacy settings.

We may also disclose data where required by law or to protect our rights and users.

7. Match screenshots & automated checks

After reporting a result, you can upload a screenshot of the Wild Rift end-of-match screen as evidence. Screenshots are stored in a private storage area that other players cannot browse; the other player in the match and our review team can see the evidence for that match.

  • What is sent. To check a result, the screenshot, together with the Riot IDs of the two players in the match, is sent to an AI model (Claude, provided by Anthropic) acting as our processor. We only send it while the match is awaiting confirmation.
  • Purpose. The model reads the player names and the result shown on the screen. We use its answer only to shorten the confirm window (to 3 minutes) when the screenshot clearly shows the reported winner and both Riot IDs, or to flag a match for our team. The winner is worked out by our own systems from your report and the names matched, not taken from the model. A screenshot never settles, cancels or disputes a match on its own, and disputes are decided by people.
  • What we keep. The screenshot, a fingerprint (hash) of the image used to detect screenshots reused across matches, and the outcome of the check (status, confidence and the names read).
  • Retention. Screenshots and check results are kept with the rest of the match record for as long as described in section 8.

8. Retention

We keep personal data only as long as needed for the purposes described here, or as required by law, after which we delete or anonymise it. Match records — including reports, room chat, screenshots and automated check results — and balance records are kept to settle disputes, prevent fraud and meet record-keeping duties. Technical error reports are deleted after 14 days. Retention periods are set out in [retention schedule].

9. Security

We apply technical and organisational measures to protect your data, including a zero-trust backend, row-level access controls, hashed credentials and encrypted transport. Match screenshots are kept in private storage, and the keys for our Riot Games and AI-model integrations are held only on our servers. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. Keeping your password and sign-in methods secure remains your responsibility.
