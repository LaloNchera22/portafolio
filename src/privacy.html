1. Who we are

Runinback ("we", "us") is a skill-based competition platform for video games, operated by [legal entity name], [registered address]. For the purposes of applicable data-protection law, the data controller is [controller name]. This policy explains how we handle personal data across our website, accounts, console and developer tools (the "Service").

2. Data we collect

Data you give us

  • Account data — username, email address, account type (player or developer) and password (stored only as a secure hash by our authentication provider).
  • Authentication data — when you sign in with Google, GitHub, Apple or Steam, we receive a basic identifier and email from that provider.
  • Communications — messages you send us through forms or email.

Data we collect automatically

  • Usage & device data — pages viewed, approximate region, browser and device type, and similar technical information.
  • Cookies & local storage — see our Cookie Policy for detail and your choices.

We do not collect your wallet private keys. During test mode, rcoin balances are simulated and recorded in our own ledger; see the Terms.

3. How we use it

  • To create and secure your account and sign you in.
  • To provide, maintain and improve the Service.
  • To communicate with you about your account, support requests and important changes.
  • To detect, prevent and investigate fraud, cheating and abuse.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

4. Legal bases

Where data-protection law requires a legal basis, we rely on: performance of a contract (to run your account and the Service), legitimate interests (to secure and improve the Service and prevent abuse), consent (for optional cookies and any marketing), and legal obligation where the law requires it. You can withdraw consent at any time.

5. Cookies

We use essential cookies to keep you signed in and to run the Service, and — only with your consent — optional cookies to understand how the Service is used. You can accept or decline optional cookies from the banner shown on your first visit, and change your choice at any time. Full detail is in our Cookie Policy.

6. Sharing & processors

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with service providers who process it on our behalf under contract, including:

  • Authentication & database — our backend provider, which stores account and profile data.
  • Hosting & content delivery — the infrastructure that serves the site.
  • Identity providers — Google, GitHub, Apple and Steam, when you choose to sign in with them.

We may also disclose data where required by law or to protect our rights and users.

7. On-chain data

When you interact with entry fee, escrow or prize payout features, transactions are recorded on a public blockchain ([target network]). On-chain data is public, permanent and outside our control; we cannot edit or erase it. Wallet addresses and transaction details may be visible to anyone. Consider this before transacting.

8. Retention

We keep personal data only as long as needed for the purposes described here, or as required by law, after which we delete or anonymise it. Retention periods are set out in [retention schedule].

9. Security

We apply technical and organisational measures to protect your data, including a zero-trust backend, row-level access controls, hashed credentials and encrypted transport. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. Protecting your own wallet keys remains your responsibility.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your personal data, to data portability, and to withdraw consent. To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below. You may also have the right to complain to your local data-protection authority.

11. International transfers

Your data may be processed in countries other than your own. Where it is transferred internationally, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as [transfer mechanism, e.g. standard contractual clauses].

12. Children

The Service is not directed to children under [minimum age], and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

13. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. We will revise the "Last updated" date and, for material changes, provide reasonable notice.

14. Contact

For privacy questions or to exercise your rights, contact privacy@runinback.com, or [data protection contact / DPO and address].
