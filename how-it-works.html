The engine

A trustless loop from pairing to payout.

No treasury. No manual payouts. No custody risk. Runinback runs every wagered match through the same verifiable four-step path, secured by an audited on-chain program.

01

Matchmaking — pair & agree

Both players connect a wallet (native Solana or an embedded custodial account) and confirm the stake, currency and match rules through a drop-in challenge overlay. Anti-collusion heuristics run before a match is ever created.

02

Smart Escrow — lock the pot

When the match starts, each stake is transferred into an audited escrow program. The funds are provably locked on-chain — neither player, nor your game, nor Runinback can move them until a verified result arrives.

03

Verification — prove the result

Your game server submits a signed result. The Runinback oracle validates the signature and opens a short dispute window backed by cryptographic evidence, so a bad actor can't fake an outcome.

04

Settlement — pay the winner

Once verified, the escrow releases the full pot to the winner automatically — typically in about a second. If a match is voided, funds are refunded to both players along the same trustless path.

Why trustless matters

You keep the players. We remove the risk.

Non-custodial by design

You never touch player funds, so you avoid money-transmitter exposure and the operational weight of a payments stack.

Verifiable on-chain

Every locked pot and payout is a public, auditable transaction. Players can confirm the stake is real before they ever queue up.

Instant & automatic

No support tickets, no batch payouts. The oracle settles the moment a result is verified, sub-second on Solana.
