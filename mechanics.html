The Trustless Engine.

Runinback operates as a neutral referee using P2P Competitive Escrow smart contracts. No human intervention, no chargebacks, just code.

1. Matchmaking & Escrow

Players connect their wallets (Solana, Polygon). The agreed wager amount is locked into an immutable smart contract. Funds cannot be moved until the match concludes.

2. Automated Settlement

Once the game ends, the game server reports the results to the Runinback Oracle. The Oracle verifies the cryptographic signatures to ensure validity.

3. Instant Payout

The contract liquidates and transfers the funds automatically to the wallet of the victor within seconds. Zero middleman fees, total transparency.
